# TAB: Take A Break

(...run away with us for the summer~ 🎶 Hamilton fans anyone?)

TAB is a chrome extension that encourages pacing yourself and taking breaks when working on screens for long periods of time. And it does so in a rather fun way!

## How to Install:
- Download our "TAB: Take A Break" extension from the Chrome Extensions Store! Click on the extensions icon and then on our timer icon to get the settings pop-up.

## How to Use:
- Use the slider under "Touch Grass Timer" to set how long you want your work period to be (up to an hour) and then toggle the "Enable TAB monitoring" to get the work timer started.
- At the end of your work period, playful messages will start popping up all over your screen encouraging you to take a break! You can still keep working around them, but they'll cover up your screen sooner rather than later!
- Once the "warning" period is over, you're in break mode! You might see a fun video pop onto your screen, and once the 5 minute break is over it's back into work mode for another round of productivity.
